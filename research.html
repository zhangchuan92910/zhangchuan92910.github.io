Research.

Understanding graph structure.
Turning characterizations into algorithms.

I work with Jing Huang on strongly chordal digraphs. My research combines structural graph theory with algorithm design: finding the patterns that characterize a graph class, then using them to recognize it efficiently.

My broader interests include quantum algorithms, computational complexity, combinatorics, and discrete optimization.

Strongly Chordal Semicomplete Digraphs

Jing Huang and Chuan Zhang

I developed forbidden-subdigraph and structural characterizations for the irreflexive case, together with a polynomial-time recognition algorithm. I also independently developed the reflexive construction; its recognition procedure reuses the irreflexive algorithm.

02

Manuscript in preparation

Strong Chordality of Locally Semicomplete Digraphs

Jing Huang and Chuan Zhang

I constructed and proved strong orderings for straight and round local tournaments, then extended these constructions to admissible tournament blowups using my supervisor’s structural framework. Current work extends the analysis to reduced multi-component cases.

With Jing Huang
